What an attacker could do beyond looking

  • Serve fake login pages for your bank or email
  • Strip HTTPS on plaintext-fallback sites
  • Inject malware into pages your browser loads over HTTP
  • Watch DNS queries to know which sites you visit, even with TLS

How to stay safer

  • Don't auto-join networks named “Free WiFi,” “Guest,” or an airport name
  • Turn off auto-connect to open networks
  • Use a VPN on networks you don't trust
  • Tether off your phone's mobile data when in doubt
  • Disable WiFi entirely when you don't need it — phones probe constantly
